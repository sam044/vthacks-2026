## ANS extension, only after the core works

Publish two team-operated prototype services, such as an access-data agent and a disruption agent, under a controlled domain. Discover and resolve the second through ANS, verify its identity under the documented protocol, then call it. Show the trace and reject an invalid identity. A hardcoded pair of URLs or an ANS logo alone does not demonstrate the challenge. Registry verification proves agent/domain identity, not the truth of its campus information or VT endorsement.

GoDaddy documents domain validation, certificates, and DNS as part of registration. Account/API eligibility must be checked early. Time-box setup; if sponsor access or DNS blocks it, protect the Deloitte demo. [ANS developer guide](https://www.godaddy.com/hi-in/ans/developers)

## Tracks to leave out of the core

- **Capital One Nessie:** natural only if selecting the basic-needs/budgeting idea; all accounts and transactions would be sandbox data.
- **Procedura:** photo-to-map-ready 3D reconstruction is a different central deliverable and too large an add-on.
- **Tiger Data / MongoDB:** a second database needs a real workload; Databricks already serves the main challenge.
- **Solana / Presage:** no necessary role in the recommended MVP.
- **CoStar / Galois:** user attachment says TBD; no invented requirements or prize mapping.
- **Best Hack That Didn't Work:** fallback recognition, not a product strategy.
- **Raffles:** follow event participation requirements separately.
